LET’S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

From pantry to plate.

  1. 1

    Prep the vegetables

    Dice the cucumber and halve the tomatoes. Drain the tuna and rinse the beans thoroughly.

  2. 2

    Shake up the dressing

    Whisk the lemon juice and olive oil with salt and pepper in a large bowl.

  3. 3

    Toss it together

    Add the tuna, beans, cucumber, tomatoes, and parsley. Fold gently to keep some texture.

  4. 4

    Serve or chill

    Divide into 4 portions. Serve immediately or refrigerate in sealed containers.

Make tomorrow a little easier

Keep refrigerated and enjoy within 3 days. Stir before serving, as the dressing may settle.
