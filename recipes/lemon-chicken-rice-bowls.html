LET’S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

From pantry to plate.

  1. 1

    Get the rice going

    Rinse the rice, then combine with water according to the package directions. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer until tender, about 15–20 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

  2. 2

    Season the chicken

    Cut the chicken into even strips. Toss with the olive oil, oregano, half the lemon juice, the lemon zest, and a pinch each of salt and pepper.

  3. 3

    Cook until golden

    Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer, cooking in batches if needed. Cook for 6–8 minutes, turning halfway, until the thickest piece reaches 165°F / 74°C.

  4. 4

    Make the lemon yogurt

    Stir the Greek yogurt with the remaining lemon juice and 1–2 tablespoons of water until drizzleable. Season to taste.

  5. 5

    Build your bowls

    Divide the rice, chicken, chickpeas, and cucumber among 4 bowls. Spoon over the lemon yogurt. Serve warm, or cool promptly and refrigerate for meal prep.

Make tomorrow a little easier

Store in sealed containers in the fridge for up to 3–4 days. Keep the cucumber and yogurt separate, and reheat the chicken and rice to 165°F / 74°C before adding them.
