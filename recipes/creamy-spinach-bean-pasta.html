Creamy Spinach & Bean Pasta.

LET’S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

From pantry to plate.

  1. 1

    Cook the pasta

    Boil the pasta according to the package directions. Reserve 1 cup of cooking water before draining.

  2. 2

    Make a creamy base

    Mash or blend half the beans with the yogurt and half a cup of pasta water.

  3. 3

    Warm the greens

    Heat oil in a large skillet. Cook the garlic for 30 seconds, add the spinach, and cook until hot throughout. Stir in the remaining beans.

  4. 4

    Bring it together

    Remove the skillet from the heat. Fold in the pasta and bean sauce, adding pasta water a splash at a time. Stir in Parmesan and season to taste.

Make tomorrow a little easier

Add the yogurt sauce off the heat to keep it smooth. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days; reheat gently with a splash of water.
